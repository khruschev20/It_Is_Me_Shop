About me

My name is Kholekile, and I am 22 years old. I live in Somerset West, Cape Town.
As someone living with schizophrenia, my aim is to create a brand that has meaning and uses creative designs to open up conversations about mental health. I want to provide another way for people to express themselves and share their experiences. I used to be a very sporty person, but due to my mental health challenges, I lost the love and motivation I once had for being active and participating in physical activities. Over time, I discovered that I was interested in having conversations about mental health. I wanted to find a creative way to express these conversations, so I opened Canva and started experimenting with different ideas. Initially, I came up with the name My Creative Centre. I then abbreviated it to MCC, and from there, everything else started to fall into place. MCC has given me direction and purpose in my life. Without it, I don't think I would have pushed myself to step outside my comfort zone. Through developing the brand, I have learned valuable skills, and on difficult days, it gives me hope and reminds me that better days are ahead. ABOUT MCC (MY CREATIVE CENTRE)

About MCC:

MCC is a personal brand with a purpose. Many people experience mental health challenges, yet finding ways to express what they are going through can be difficult. MCC aims to stand alongside everyone facing a mental health challenge, raise awareness and offer hope through creative expression. Some people find it difficult to communicate their feelings verbally. MCC provides an alternative way to express emotions, experiences and personal struggles through creative designs and clothing. A percentage of the profits will go towards a mental health charity, helping to make counselling more accessible to people who need it. I have found great value in counselling and know that access to counselling and mental health support in South Africa can play an important role in preventing mental health crises and reducing emotional distress. THE MEANING BEHIND MCC The words "IT IS MCC", featured in the brand's logo, represent an important message: I am still me. I am still a human being, a son, a brother and a friend. No matter what challenges I face or what diagnosis I have received, that does not define who I am. We all express ourselves creatively. My way of expressing myself might look different from yours, but that doesn't make me any different from you. Anything is possible. THE FUTURE OF MCC In the future, I hope MCC will make a meaningful impact on people's lives by spreading awareness about mental health and providing a creative way for people to express the things they struggle to put into words. My vision is to help make conversations about mental health normal, open and accepted — something we should never be afraid to talk about. I also want MCC to educate people, encourage greater understanding and empathy, and help people understand one another better. Ultimately, I hope to contribute towards making the world a more understanding, supportive and inclusive place for everyone.
